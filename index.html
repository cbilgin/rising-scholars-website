About

A real engineer, not just a tutor

Math is easier to teach well when you've used it for a living. My tutoring is built on knowing why the math matters and how to make it click.

I'm an engineer with a Ph.D., an M.S. and a B.S. in engineering and a background as a big-tech software engineer. That means students get explanations grounded in how math is really used, not just a list of procedures to memorize.

I also write my own teaching materials: lesson sheets, study guides, practice worksheets and original math games, so every session is tailored to the student in front of me rather than pulled off a shelf.

  • Fun for little kids, rigorous for big kidsGame-based math in K–5; careful, proof-minded work in middle and high school.
  • Patient with kids who struggleNo rushing, no judgment. We find the exact gap and build from there.
  • Room to go furtherHigh-cap students get enrichment and problem solving that goes beyond grade level.
